Can a Bee Understand Zero?

Can a bee count? What about counting to zero?

We thought zero was an idea only big brains could grasp. Then researchers in Australia put honeybees to the test.

They trained the bees with sugar water. The rule was simple. Pick the card with fewer shapes. Then, on test day, the bees saw a blank card for the first time. And they picked it. Even next to a card with just one shape, they treated the empty one as fewer, sixty-four percent of the time. And the bigger the gap, the fewer mistakes they made. Just like us.

We can't read a bee's mind. But the researchers say this means bees place zero on the number line, below one. With a brain of about a million neurons.

An idea it took humans thousands of years to find was already buzzing in a bee's head.
